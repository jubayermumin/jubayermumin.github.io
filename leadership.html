Guidance, Recovery, and Overcoming Wound (GROW)

Aug 2024 – Present

A research project following the student-led revolution in Bangladesh in July 2024.

Phase 1 Objectives:
  • To assess the prevalence and severity of trauma and other psychiatric symptoms.
  • To identify coping or resilience measures.
  • To compare the severity of trauma across different generations (Gen X/Millennial vs. Gen Z/Alpha).
Phase 2 Objective:
  • To identify the role and impact of formal and informal support systems in helping individuals process trauma.
