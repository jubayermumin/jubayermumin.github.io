I have recently completed my master’s in Public Health Epidemiology at Karolinska Institute in Sweden. I am a medical doctor with nine years' experience in research, public health response, and clinical medicine. My research area primarily focuses on epidemiology, mental health, and infectious diseases.

I have had the privilege of working with international NGOs and UN agencies, contributing to nutrition and health interventions in diverse settings. A defining moment in my career was leading and managing the COVID-19 vaccination campaigns in the world's largest refugee camps, the Rohingya refugee camps in Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh.

With a strong commitment to health equity and advocacy, my expertise lies in capacity building and empowering communities to respond effectively to health crises.
