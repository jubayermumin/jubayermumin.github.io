I have recently completed my master’s in Public Health Epidemiology at Karolinska Institute in Sweden. I am a medical doctor with nine years' experience in research, public health response, and clinical medicine. My research area primarily focuses on epidemiology, mental health, and infectious diseases.

I have had the privilege of working with international NGOs and UN agencies, contributing to nutrition and health interventions in diverse settings. A defining moment in my career was leading and managing the COVID-19 vaccination campaigns in the world's largest refugee camps, the Rohingya refugee camps in Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh. This hands-on experience not only honed my leadership skills but also ignited a deep sense of purpose in addressing health disparities faced by displaced populations.

With a strong commitment to health equity and advocacy, my expertise lies in capacity building and empowering communities to respond effectively to health crises. I have actively participated in outbreak investigations and response efforts, leveraging my knowledge to help control and manage disease outbreaks.

Immunization in humanitarian emergency is another key aspect of my proficiency, ensuring that even in the most challenging circumstances, life-saving vaccinations are administered to those in need.

A non-exhaustive list of topics that I am interested about:
  • Real world evidence
  • Causal inference
  • Psychiatric epidemiology
  • Global mental health
  • Infectious disease epidemiology
  • Nutrition and breastfeeding
  • Information Theory
  • Survival analysis
